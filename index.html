ABOUT

Understanding quantum materials
through computation.

I currently work as a Research Software Engineer (RSE) at DARSE.

I earned my Ph.D. in Physics and Astronomy at the University of Delaware (2018–2025). My research explores open quantum systems, ultrafast dynamics, and quantum–classical hybrid simulations, grounded in nonequilibrium many-body physics.

I have developed models of terahertz emission from magnetic materials, investigated many-body excited states in spintronic systems, and quantified electron-mediated entanglement between distant ferromagnets.
