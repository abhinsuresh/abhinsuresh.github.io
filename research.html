Quantum transport & spin dynamics

Developed a self-consistent quantum–classical framework combining time-dependent nonequilibrium Green-function (TDNEGF) theory with the Landau–Lifshitz–Gilbert equation. This work investigates how precessing magnetic moments pump charge and spin currents and how magnetic materials transmit spin currents.

Recast the TDNEGF integro-differential equation as linear differential equations with time-linear scaling. Also implemented neural networks to predict time-evolution errors for a truncated wavefunction in 2D TDNEGF.

Python · Fortran · TDNEGF · LLG · Scikit-learn

Quantum many-body effects & entanglement

Developed numerical tools to test classical descriptions of quantum spins and the Holstein–Primakoff mapping. Used quantum trajectories with MPI/OpenMP across 1,024 processing elements to evolve systems coupled to multiple thermal baths.

Quantified long-range dynamical entanglement between distant macroscopic ferromagnets in a spin valve and proposed an experimental method to witness it.

Quantum trajectories · Sparse matrices · MPI/OpenMP · Entanglement

Ultrafast magnetism & terahertz emission

Developed a quantum–classical framework coupled to Maxwell’s equations to study ultrafast-light-driven dynamics in the Weyl antiferromagnet Mn₃Sn. Investigated microscopic mechanisms of terahertz and high-harmonic emission and implemented multithreaded scans of emitted radiation.

Julia · MPI · Electromagnetism · Multiscale modeling

First-principles spin pumping

Studied spin pumping from antiferromagnetic insulators using ab initio density functional theory and Floquet-NEGF. Extracted spin mixing conductance parameters and validated results against TDNEGF–LLG simulations.

QuantumATK · DFT · Floquet-NEGF · Regression
