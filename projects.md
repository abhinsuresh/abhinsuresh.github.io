Research Assistant, Ph.D. Candidate
University of Delaware      2019 – present
Developed advanced mathematical models and numerical frameworks to simulate the time-dependent, self-consistent interaction between conduction electrons and localized magnetic moments in dissipative environments.
Models include 
1.  Quantum-classical hybrid using time-dependent non-equilibrium Greens function theory and Landau-Lifshitz-Gilbert (LLG) equations.
2.  Fully quantum using density matrix renormalization group and exact diagonalization.
3.  Coupled electromagnetic interaction using the method of Jefimenko’s equations.

- This work provided a detailed Hamiltonian-based understanding of key phenomena, including:
1.  Microscopic mechanisms behind terahertz emission from laser-irradiated magnetic bilayers.
2.  Dynamics of Magnons/spin wave in spintronics systems.
3.  Time-dependent non-equilibrium quantum many body states and Green’s functions. 
4.  Long-range dynamical entanglement in magnetic materials.
5.  Validation of bosonic mapping of quantum spin operators and classical treatment of quantum spin systems.
6.  Substantiated experimental observation of laser polarization and sample rotation dependence on terahertz emission from magnetic bilayer.
7.  Implemented parallel programming methods in Julia for parameter scans and to scan the emitted radiation on the surface of a sphere centered around the material.
